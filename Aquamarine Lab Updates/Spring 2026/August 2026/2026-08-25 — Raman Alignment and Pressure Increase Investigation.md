We added a beam sampler in Raman path for intensity stabilization - which caused the beam to be misaligned from the ion location. So we used the Rods and Needles to align it back into the trap center. But while doing that we left the Raman beam ON, going in to the vacuum chamber, and after the alignment was complete we saw the pressure had rise by two orders of magnitude. Since the pressure was high anyway we installed and turned on the pressure gauge and then pressure increased by another order of magnitude (the gauge reading at the time of installation was $\sim$ 1e-7 Torr), over the next two days pressure went back down to 1e-10 Torr to 1e-11 Torr range - hard to tell because pressure gauge behaves weird at these pressures, but ion pump supply is drawing 0-1 nA (decreased from $\sim$ 200 nA). 

After further investigation we found that we had scorched the FPC on the bridge that carries needle potential across the holder. The following are images from that investigation. These pictures were taken with iPhone 15 pro and an endoscope.
