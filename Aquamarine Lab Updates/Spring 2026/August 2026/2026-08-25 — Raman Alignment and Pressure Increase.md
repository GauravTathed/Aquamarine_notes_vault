So we used the Rods and Needles to align it back into the trap center. Since the pressure was high anyway we installed and turned open the pressure gauge and then pressure instead by another order of magnitude, over the next two days pressure went back down to 1e-10 to 1e-11 range - hard to tell because pressure gauge behaves weird at these pressures. 

Maybe for the next time when we do this use an iris in front of the high power beam and don't keep thee beam on the rods or needs for the long.
